### 1b. What kept the seam, and what took the obligation

**The seam is doctrine and it stays.** LANGUAGES.md §10: *"the SEAM is the whole divider vocabulary"* and
*"the structure device is the cell FACE a character is flipped onto, so the language spends no rule at all
and keeps its strongest divider unspent"*. `▁` is spent 137 more times on that same screen — the row seam,
the head rule, and the DEFAULT rung of every control's chrome ladder (`▁` default, `▔` focused, `▂`
active, `╌` dead). **That is ALPHABET**, and B×B is not a collision by this batch's own rule. What moved
is the one seat where the seam had to carry a MEANING.

**Obligation takes `▮` — the flap standing.** §10 again: *"the structure device is the cell FACE a
character is flipped onto."* `▮` is that face caught UPRIGHT: a cell standing with nothing flipped onto it
yet. It is this language's own caret, which is **the same claim from the other side** — the caret says the
next character lands HERE, the obligation says a character MUST land here. One meaning at two seats, which
is the inverse of the defect this batch exists to remove.

**Why not the two the brief offered.** `▔` is the FOCUSED rung of the chrome ladder and `▂` the ACTIVE
one, so an obligation drawn with either would have opened a focused or a pressed control — inc48's law,
and the same defect one increment early. `═` is `DISCLOSE` **and** the INVALID mark, so it is a meaning
already. A printed mark (`CNX`'s family) is ruled out by the seat itself:
`test_a_required_mark_is_one_cell_and_costs_no_rationed_hue` holds `len(plain(k.required())) == 1` in all
eleven, and a departure board's own answer to obligation would be a WORD, which this seat cannot hold.
**That is a real tension between the seat and the language, and it is named rather than resolved.**

**And solari went 6 → 0 on inc46's named-seat roster**, because `▁` was also the switch's INDICATOR.
Fixing the obligation fixed that seat without touching the switch.

## 2. nord — the load plot stops beating the subject

### 2a. The metric is the language's own

`Nord.__doc__` carries the measurement that makes this a defect rather than a preference:

> *"colour-stripped at 118×30 nord had NO first fixation … the only isolated element, the hero numeral,
> came FIFTH behind the load plot standing beside it in the same panel"*

The split (`layout="split"`) is this language's ONE commitment and it exists to answer exactly that: *"the
split gives the eye one subject"*. `nord_S1` reproduced the same failure one layout later — the plot
beating the declared subject in the pane the split had just created.

```
before  ▸   Fix login redirect       3d  │ ▇▇▇▇▇▇▇▇▇▇▇▇░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  44%
after   ▸   Fix login redirect       3d  │  44% ━━━━━━━━━━━━───────────────
```

### 2b. Two seats, one mechanism — and the second one is where the metric was taken

**`_meter_blocks` is the base's**, and it draws for exactly one language: the other ten override the
`meter` token (`dotgrid`, `lcd`, `braille`, `hairline`, `boxed`, `step`, `ember`, `tally`, `odometer`,
`dimension`) and `blocks` is what is left. **`Nord.detail_rows` draws a second one inline**, and that is
the one the docstring measured — the frame's bar comes from `screens.py` calling `k.meter()`, the live
split's from `detail_rows`. Fixing only the base would have moved the photograph and left the app.

Both now draw the same pair. **What replaces the blocks is not an invention:** Rich — the library this app
is built on — draws its own progress bar with `━`, so a terminal that has a bar has that one. The complete
run is the heavy rule and the remainder is the light one, so the two still differ in SHAPE and greyscale
holds, at roughly a seventh of the ink COVERAGE. **And the figure leads the meter's row**, because DATAVIZ
law 1 is that a gauge STATES its value and because the first cell of a row is the one the eye reaches
first — which is the quantity the docstring measured.

## 3. The law that moved, and the one that learned something

**`verify_language.py`** carried a check that named the mechanism by its glyphs: *"nord: quantity in the
detail is nord's OWN meter family (the filled/unfilled block pair `_meter_blocks` draws)"*. It is

**`meaning_marks_at_named_seats` learned that a fallback is not a declaration**, and it learned it from a
false positive this increment created. `part_glyph` walks the state chain, so a part with no `disabled`

CARET was reported as a "disabled mark". A field nobody may type in draws no caret at all. The census had
to learn the identical lesson in inc44 and its comment says so; this is the second time and now both
instruments say it. **naught's count fell 9 → 8 on the same fix**, which is the correction the false
positive was hiding.

`MEANING_AT_A_NAMED_SEAT` is now `naught 8 · corgi 8 · prism 8 · blueprint 8` and **zero for the other

## 7. Risks

- **`▮` is `REQUIRED` and the caret in the same row.** `solari_S2` row 4 reads
  `title▮        ▔Fix login▮ redirect…` — the same cell twice, three cells apart. The argument that this
  is ONE meaning at two seats is written at the declaration and is the strongest available, but a reader
  who does not hold that argument sees a repeat. It is the same shape of cost `naught ◉` carries
  (inc45 §7) and it is the second time this batch has had to spend it, which is worth noticing: two of
  the eleven have no unspent cell left.
- **A departure board's honest answer to obligation is a WORD**, and the seat holds one cell. `LEVELS` is
  three printed cells in this language for exactly that reason; `REQUIRED` cannot be, so this is the one
  place solari's doctrine is bent by the contract rather than by taste.
- **Nord's bar lost a lot of weight and the accent stayed on it.** `━` in the accent hue over `─` in dim
  is a thinner reading than `▇` over `░` was, and on a terminal that renders `━` at one pixel of height
  the bar may now be *too* quiet. The metric it was changed to satisfy is about not out-weighing the
  subject, and there is no measurement in this repo for "loud enough".
- **`Nord.detail_rows` still draws its own bar inline** rather than calling `self.meter()`. Two seats now
  agree by having the same two glyphs typed twice, which is the kind of agreement that drifts. Making
  one call the other is a composition change and was not in this increment's scope.
