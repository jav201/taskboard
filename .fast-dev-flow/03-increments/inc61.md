## 4. `·` is retired, and the three reasons are asserted rather than recited

inc45 declared naught's charge ramp as `⋅ · ◦ ∙ ◉ ●`, six rungs. Two of the six were `·` and `∙`.

1. **`·` is the HOMOGLYPH of `∙`**, and `∙` carries the `DANGER_FORM` and two severity rungs. `("·","∙")`
   is in `collision_census.HOMOGLYPHS` by name, and ruling D forbids telling a MEANING from its CHROME
   that way. **Both of naught's homoglyph rows were this one pair**, read in both directions.
2. **§0 had already measured it**: *"`•` (U+2022), `·` (U+00B7) and `●` (U+25CF) measured and rejected"*.

   and width safety is the property that pass chose the pixel pair for (*"both EAW Neutral, safer than
   the Ambiguous block pair they replace"*). Asserted in the test with `unicodedata`.

**The ramp is five rungs now: `⋅ ◦ ∙ ◉ ●`.**
