# inc85 — Q1 over K6: twenty-six seats leave the quiet tier

Batch `rework-8c`, increment 1 of 2.

## §0 — the rulings, verbatim

Rulings (orchestrator, 2026-09-07, on the operator's delegation):

- **swiss `•`:** the floor stays at 15 %; swiss's obligation mark is exempt by name (`SEEN_BY_EYE`)
  with round five's eye-read as the evidence and the human session as the review that can revoke it.
  Neither the floor nor the mark moves.
- **Q1 over K6 for marks:** Q1 governs meaning marks (runs of 1–4 cells carrying an A-family role);
  K6 governs text runs. Where a meaning mark is painted in `mut` or `dim` and fails Q1's effective
  contrast at its declared seat, the mark takes `ink` at that seat (inc74's `log_row` precedent),
  never a token move. Decorative seats (Q2's ≥ 8 structure and the named 5–7 table) are untouched.
- **L12:** a hue-only match channel that falls under 3:1 in grey is a Limit of that language, recorded
  in the kit docstring and in `spec.md`, not fixed.

This increment is the **second** ruling. The first and third are inc86.

## §1 — cause

inc81 measured 89 bound seats and found 41 under the floor. **Twenty-nine of the forty-one missed
one clause only — the effective one — and inc81 said in writing that nobody had ruled on which floor
won:**

> K6 asks `mut` for **4.5:1 declared**; a thin glyph at 4.5:1 declared lands near **2:1 effective**.
> Q1 is strictly the harder floor for anything that is not solid.

Both floors were green in the same repo at the same time and they disagreed about the same cells.
That is not a bug in either; it is two rulings written three batches apart, one about a COLOUR PAIR
and one about a DRAWING, and the corpus needed a sentence saying which one a mark answers to.

## §3 — mechanism

Two mechanisms, one file, and **no token moved** — `mut` is still `mut` and `dim` is still `dim` in
all eleven, which is inc74's precedent for the third time.

### The rungs — `language.RUNG_TAKES_INK`

`log_row`'s tone was `mut / mut / ink` by level. It is now `mut / mut / ink` **except** at the seats
a module-level roster names, with the measurement per row:

```python
RUNG_TAKES_INK = {"swiss": ("warn",), "industrial": ("info", "warn"),
                  "nord": ("warn",), "darkside": ("warn",), "prism": ("warn",),
                  "ledger": ("warn",), "solari": ("info", "warn"),
                  "blueprint": ("warn",)}
```

**A roster and not a rule, because `language.py` cannot measure pixels.** Whether a rung clears 3:1
effective depends on the GLYPH — industrial's `▪` clears it and its `▫` does not, at the same tier on
the same ground — so the decision has to arrive as a declaration with its number attached. The three
kits that are not in it are not in it for three different reasons and none of them is "it passed
silently": corgi clears the clause in `mut` at both rungs (4.77, 6.46); naught and instrument miss
the COVERAGE clause too, and a tone cannot add area.

**The MESSAGE did not move.** An info row's words are still `mut`. That is the other half of the same
ruling — a message is a TEXT RUN and K6 is the floor written for it — and it is the sentence that
keeps this increment from being "paint everything `ink`".

### The walls — `Kit.field_wall_tone`

`textfield`, `textarea` and `select` all lay the same two walls and all three read
`part_tone("main", state, "textfield")` for them, which is `dim` in every state. One method now
stands in front of all three:

```python
def field_wall_tone(self, state, name="textfield"):
    if control_of(state) == INVALID:
        return self.c["ink"]
    return self.part_tone("main", state, name)
```

**A wall is not paper when it is the refusal.** Every other state's walls are the field's chrome —
the ground someone else's words lie on, drawn one tier under them. The INVALID walls are the only
thing on the row that says THIS VALUE WAS REFUSED, `role_map` has credited them to the `invalid`
family since the census existed, and **all eleven missed the clause**: `═` at 1.11, `░` at 1.05, `╲`
at 1.14, `◑` and `Ø` at 1.18 — a refusal mark drawn at the level of the page it refuses on.

**The PAPER keeps `dim`**, and that is the ruling's "decorative seats are untouched" applied where it
bites: the run of `⋅` / `┆` / `░` / `·` between the walls is the field's ground, Q2 classifies it as
structure, and only the walls changed tier. A rule spelled at three call sites is a rule that will be
true at two of them, so it is one method.

## §4 — the law and the teeth

**`test_no_meaning_mark_that_only_misses_the_contrast_clause_is_left_quiet` (×11)** — the ruling's own
sentence turned into something that can go red: no meaning mark whose only failure at its declared
seat is Q1's effective clause is still painted in `mut` or `dim`. Judged on the SHIPPED PIXELS
(`floor_rows` reads the PNGs) with the tier read off the theme, and **symmetric**: every `EFF`-only
row of the corpus is named in `EFFECTIVE_UNCURED` and every named row must still be one.

**`test_every_seat_the_ruling_moved_is_drawn_in_ink_and_measured_there`** — all twenty-six, from both
ends at once. The tier comes from the kit (`declared_seat_tones` renders the contract method), the
numbers from the pixels (`_drawn` re-measures the glyph on that kit's ground at the tier it left and
the tier it took). Four clauses: the seat is in `ink` today; both recorded numbers reproduce to two
places; the move is upward and the `before` was under the clause except at the one seat
`CARRIED_BY_THE_LADDER` names; the move CLEARS the clause except at the seats `EFFECTIVE_UNCURED`
names — **so a seat that moved and did not help cannot be counted as a fix.** Non-vacuous by
construction: the invalid mechanism must be exercised in all eleven, and the rung mechanism in
exactly the kits `RUNG_TAKES_INK` declares.

**`test_the_uncured_table_covers_what_it_claims_to`** — the roster's vacuity arms: all three kinds
used, `no louder neutral` rows asserted to BE in `ink`, `a hue` rows asserted NOT to be a neutral of
their kit.

**`test_the_info_rung_is_air_by_doctrine_or_legible_by_tier`** amended, not replaced: L7's sentence is
unchanged ("air with a citation, or a tier a reader has") and `ink` is emphatically such a tier. The
tier still comes from the kit and the verdict from the suite, so **a kit in `RUNG_TAKES_INK` that
kept drawing its info rung in `mut` goes red.**

**Teeth — `test_the_ink_move_bites_when_one_seat_keeps_its_mut`.** `swiss` is removed from
`RUNG_TAKES_INK`, its warn rung falls back to `mut`, and the moved-seat law must go red while the
other ten keep their `ink` pairs. **The arm had to be found rather than picked:** blueprint's warn and
error are both `━`, and nord's and ledger's warn rungs are the error glyph with a space, so reverting
any of those three leaves the `(family, cell, ink)` triple standing on the ERROR rung and the mutant
passes with the seat quiet. swiss's ladder is `· / ─ / ━`, three distinct cells, so removing its row
removes exactly one triple. That is written into the tooth's docstring, because the obvious arm was
the silent one.

## §5 — what was found by looking

**1. One of the six survivors is not a mark at all, and it is prose.** After the move,
`darkside severity o #757575` is still under the clause — but its RUNG moved and measures 8.34. What
the row measures is the letter `o` of `form`, `log` and `fix login redirect`: darkside is **the only
kit of eleven whose severity rung is a LOWERCASE LETTER**, so `role_map` credits every `o` in the
corpus to the severity family and `declared_seat_tones` picks up the log MESSAGE, which `log_row`
still sets in `mut` because a message is a text run. `legibility.py`'s own docstring claims the
intersection with `role_map` prevents exactly this (*"a letter that happens to sit inside a log
message is not promoted to a severity rung"*) and it does not, because `role_map` is keyed by
CHARACTER. **Before this increment the defect was invisible**: the rung produced the same
`(o, mut)` pair, so the row looked like a rung. It is recorded with that reason and handed back as
an instrument question — a derivation change is not what this ruling authorised.

**2. Two seats moved and were not cured, and both are facts about the DRAWING.** `corgi ░` goes from
1.05 to **1.44** in `ink` at 17.36:1 declared: `░` is a DITHER, and at 9×19 its lit pixels are almost
all partway back to the ground, so 68.4 % coverage carries a mean colour a page-width off the ink it
was asked for. `ledger *` goes from 1.91 to **2.86** — onto the row its own error rung already
occupied — on the corpus's one light ground, where `ink` is the loudest neutral there is. **No tier
fixes either; only another cell would**, and that is a glyph decision for a round.

**3. The bound-seat count FELL, 89 → 86, and that is arithmetic and not attrition.** A seat drawn in
two tones is two rows; `ledger *`, `nord !` and `darkside o` each collapsed to one when the quiet
copy moved onto the loud one. It is asserted beside the failing count precisely so a seat that stops
being DRAWN cannot be mistaken for a seat that passed.

**4. The gallery is not immune this time, and two grids moved.** Every `rework-8` increment could
report *"gallery 30–51: none changed byte-wise"* because the board frames draw no obligation mark, no
modal and no key bar. **They do draw an invalid field** — in two of the eleven — so
`gallery_darkside.svg` and `gallery_solari.svg` changed, and no other. Counted by looking for the
mark rather than assumed from the last four increments.

**5. `instrument ⠶` is the one kit where the wall and the paper are the SAME GLYPH**, so the split
this increment makes is visible inside a single field: `⠶` now appears in `ink` at both ends and in
`dim` for the six paper cells between them, from one contract call. It is the cleanest demonstration
in the corpus that what moved is a SEAT and not a token — the same cell, two tiers, two jobs.

**6. The `⠶` seat's run classes settle the "decorative seats untouched" clause by themselves.** Before
the split it was one row with runs 1–27; the `dim` half is now a run of paper that Q2 reads as
structure and drops from the bound set, and the `ink` half is two runs of one. Nothing had to be
exempted by name for that to be true.

## §7 — deviations, named

- **The law is narrower than the brief's one-line statement, on purpose.** The brief writes it as *"no
  meaning mark at its declared seat is painted in a tone under Q1's floor"*. As shipped it is *"no
  meaning mark whose ONLY failure is the effective clause is painted in `mut` or `dim`"*. The eight
  seats that miss BOTH clauses are all in `mut` and would violate the wider wording; **moving them
  would cure nothing** (their failure is area) and would spend a tier for no reading. They are named
  in `BELOW_THE_FLOOR` with `COVEFF` and the boundary is written into the table's comment. If the
  round wants the wider wording, the move is one line and the eight rows are already enumerated.
- **The three cursors in `accent` were not touched**, and the refusal is in `EFFECTIVE_UNCURED` with a
  reason each rather than in a filter. The ruling names `mut` and `dim`; `accent` is neither, and a
  cursor family that fails as a FAMILY (2.94, 2.57, 2.97 — one of them three hundredths under) is a
  round's question, not an increment's.
- **`industrial ▪` moved without a measurement condemning it.** Named above and in
  `CARRIED_BY_THE_LADDER`. It is the only such row and the law asserts it is the only such row.
- **The `darkside o` derivation defect was recorded, not fixed.** Changing `A_SEAT_CALLS` to exclude
  the log message would delete a red row in the same increment that would benefit from its absence —
  `rework-7c`'s founding principle — and it is a Q3 question rather than a Q1-over-K6 one.
- **`collision_census.py` is unchanged and its numbers are unchanged** (TOTAL 27, homoglyph rows 24).
  It reads DECLARATIONS; a tier is not a declaration it counts.
