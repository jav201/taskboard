## §1 — cause

The eight rows that missed **both** of Q1's clauses are round five's own ten-worst list, minus the two
inc82 fixed. They are **six severity ladders**, and five of them are the CALM rung — the mark that
says *nothing has gone wrong*:


and 49.1 % — are **all larger than this ladder's gravest rung**. A calm rung heavier than the grave

**What was available was the tier, and it was taken.** naught joins `RUNG_TAKES_INK`, `◦` measures
**5.68** instead of 2.40, and the seat misses **one** clause instead of two. It stays in
