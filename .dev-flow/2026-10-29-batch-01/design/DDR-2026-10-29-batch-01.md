## Lesson — why the tests missed O1 and O2

The tests were not vacuous; they asserted the wrong things. AT-3104 and TC-3112 assert the document width and the gantt against the viewport only; `.tbl-wrap` clips, so `scrollWidth` stays small and the table's clipping never reddens a test. TC-3112 never asserts the scope-line board names or "Projects 2/6", although PDR criterion 2 and LLR-3104.1 name them. Rule for the next report-like batch: for every PDR criterion that names printed or exported text, the node asserts that text (and the absence of the forbidden text) in the artefact the recipient receives, with a fixture that hides something; a bound on an enclosing box does not bound a clipped child.
