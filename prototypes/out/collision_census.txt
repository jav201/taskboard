COLLISION CENSUS — one cell, more than one job
==============================================================================

A cell is listed when it carries roles from two or more A-families
(severity, danger, required, invalid, cursor), or from an A-family and a
control's chrome. Chrome-only sharing between two controls is a
language's alphabet and is counted but not listed. Read the module
docstring of prototypes/collision_census.py for the argument.

HOMOGLYPHS — one drawing at another size or fill (ruling D amended)
==============================================================================
Ruling D (2026-09-06): a channel is COUNT, WEIGHT, POSITION or
DIRECTION. Diameter alone is none of them, so a language that tells a
MEANING apart from its own CHROME -- or from another MEANING -- by
size or fill alone has told them apart with nothing. Ruling D amended
(2026-09-07): the pairs are DERIVED from these families, any two
members of one row being one drawing. Triangles are NOT a family:
they differ by DIRECTION, which the D-addendum rules a channel.

    ◦ o ○ ◎ ◉ ⊙ ⊛ O
    ▫ □ ▪ ■
    † ‡
    ╌ ┄ ┈
    -> 48 pairs
These rows are NOT added to the counts above: a homoglyph row is a
question about two drawings, and the count is a question about one
cell.
